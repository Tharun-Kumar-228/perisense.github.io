About PeriSense

PeriSense is a startup-driven smart goods monitoring system designed to ensure the safety, quality, and integrity of perishable and high-value goods throughout storage and transportation.

The Problem We Solve

  • Temperature and humidity fluctuations
  • Undetected gas emissions and spoilage
  • Lack of real-time visibility during transit
  • Manual monitoring and delayed interventions

Our Solution

  • Temperature monitoring
  • Humidity tracking
  • Gas emission detection
  • Storage and transit status monitoring

Key Features

IoT-Based Sensor Network

Deployable wireless sensors that continuously monitor environmental conditions in real-time.

Real-Time Dashboard

Comprehensive analytics platform with visualization tools for supply chain visibility.

Smart Alerts & Notifications

Instant alerts via SMS, email, or app when conditions deviate from preset thresholds.

AI-Driven Insights

Predictive analytics to forecast potential issues before they impact your goods (Roadmap).

Secure & Scalable Architecture

Enterprise-grade security with cloud infrastructure that scales with your operations.
