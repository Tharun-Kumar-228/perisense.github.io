Our Mission

About PeriSense

PeriSense is dedicated to revolutionizing the agriculture supply chain. We create intelligent sensors that protect fresh produce, reducing the billions lost annually to spoilage and ensuring healthy, high-quality food reaches communities worldwide.

The Challenge

Why Freshness Fades

40% of food is wasted before it hits the shelf.

Field Heat & Temp Spikes

Improper cooling after harvest accelerates decay in sensitive crops like berries and leafy greens.

Ethylene Buildup

Invisible ripening gases can cause entire shipments of fruits to spoil overnight.

In-Transit Delays

Lack of visibility during shipping leads to rejected loads and financial loss.
